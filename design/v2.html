How it works

Like splitting a receipt, but nobody does the math.

  1. Household

    Invite your roommates

    Send one private link. It expires, and you can cancel it any time.

  2. Bill

    Tear it into shares

    Split evenly or by custom amounts. Every cent lands on a stub, so the shares always add up to the total.

  3. Settle

    Stamp it paid

    Your roommate stamps it "reported paid". You stamp it "confirmed received". That's the whole system.

Fine print

What it does and doesn't do.

Does eqlShare move money?

No. Pay each other the way you already do. eqlShare just keeps the record.

Who sees our bills?

Only active members of your household. Other households can't see anything.

What about leftover cents?

$100.00 split three ways comes to $33.34, $33.33 and $33.33. The extra cent goes to the same person every time.

What if someone moves out?

Their past stubs stay on the record. New bills leave them out by default.
