House rules.

Does eqlShare handle the money?

No. There are no bank links, no cards and no transfers. You pay each other however you like, and eqlShare keeps a record everyone agrees on.

How are odd cents split?

$137.01 split three ways is $45.67 each. $100.00 split three ways is $33.34, $33.33 and $33.33, and the extra cent goes to the same person every time.

Can my landlord or a stranger see our bills?

No. Only household members can see them, and access is checked on the server for every request.

What if I disagree with my share?

Mark it disputed and leave a note. The history stays as it was until someone edits the bill, and every edit is logged.
