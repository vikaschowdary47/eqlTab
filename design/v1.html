    Built to answer one question: who owes what?

    We keep track. We don't touch your money.

    eqlShare is a shared record for your household. You still pay each other however you already do.

    • No bank connections, cards or money transfers.
    • Only members can see a household's bills. Every check happens on the server.
    • We never say a transfer is verified. A person confirms it.
    • Export your records whenever you want, including after you move out.

    Questions

    What happens to odd cents?

    $100.00 split three ways is $33.34, $33.33 and $33.33. The extra cent always goes to the same person, in member order, so the shares add up every time.

    Someone moved out. Do we lose the history?

    No. Old bills and balances stay as they were. New bills leave that person out by default.

    Does the Internet bill copy last month's amount?

    No. Each month you get a draft to check before anyone is charged.

    Is it free?

    It's free during the closed beta. We haven't set pricing yet.
